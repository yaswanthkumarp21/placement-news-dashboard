# Privacy Policy: Placement News

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Placement News is a small, free, non-commercial news dashboard for MBA placement preparation, run by a student for a study group.

## What we collect
When you sign in with Google we receive only your **name and email address**. We also store what you do in the app: which companies you turned off, the role you picked, and the news items you saved.

## What we use it for
Only to sign you in and to show you your own settings and saved news. We do not sell, share or advertise with your data. We do not use it for anything else.

## Where it is stored
In a Supabase database (supabase.com). Other users cannot see your saved items or settings. The site administrator can see the list of signed-in users and can remove a user.

## Cookies
Only the sign-in session cookie needed to keep you logged in.

## Deleting your data
Ask the administrator to remove your account (open an issue at https://github.com/yaswanthkumarp21/placement-news-dashboard/issues). Removing your account deletes your profile, settings and saved items.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
