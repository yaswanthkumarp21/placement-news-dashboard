### [2026-01-17] DGCA fines IndiGo ₹22.2 cr for the December 2025 crew-rostering meltdown; orders reforms
- **Recap:** DGCA's probe into the December 2025 disruption found that over three days 2,507 flights were cancelled and 1,852 delayed, affecting over 300,000 passengers. Root causes: rosters that maximised crew duty periods, heavy dead-heading, no buffers for the revised flight duty time limitation (FDTL) rules, and software gaps. The penalty was ₹22.20 cr (₹1.80 cr for CAR violations and ₹20.40 cr for 68 days of FDTL non-compliance), plus a ₹50 cr bank guarantee under a reform-assurance scheme. The CEO was cautioned, the COO warned, and the SVP of the operations control centre removed.
- **Source:** [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/indigo-flight-disruptions-dgca-slaps-over-rs22-cr-penalty-orders-sweeping-reforms-after-december-chaos-511349-2026-01-17); additional: [Wikipedia: 2025 IndiGo scheduling crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_IndiGo_scheduling_crisis)
- **Source quality:** Regulator order via business press
- **Confidence:** Corroborated
- **Theme:** Regulatory & compliance / Workforce planning
- **Role relevance:** Operations planning / Service operations
- **Why it matters:** **The single best operations case in this run.** A capacity-planning failure: crew was the binding constraint, and the plan ran with zero slack when a rule change cut available duty hours. Expect "how would you build a crew roster that survives a regulation change" — buffer crew, scenario planning, roster optimisation software with rule checks, and reserve ratios.
- **Key numbers:** 2,507 cancellations; 1,852 delays; 300,000+ passengers; ₹22.20 cr penalty; ₹50 cr guarantee.

## Likely operations interview questions

1. **"What went wrong in IndiGo's December crisis and how would you prevent it?"** Crew was the constraint; build reserve crew, model regulation changes early, use rule-checked rostering software, set buffer KPIs.
2. **"Fuel cost jumps 80%. What do you do?"** Park least-efficient aircraft, cut marginal routes, hedge, optimise flight plans and tankering, raise fares where demand allows.
3. **"Single vs dual sourcing for engines?"** Dual sourcing reduces disruption risk but adds spares and training complexity; weigh reliability, lease cost and MRO capability.
4. **"How would you set up a hub at Delhi?"** Banked waves, minimum connection times, transfer baggage, slot availability, ground handling capacity.
5. **"How do you recover a network after airspace closure?"** Prioritise high-load routes, re-position aircraft and crew, communicate early, rebook proactively.
6. **"How do you decide to return leased aircraft?"** Compare lease cost with utilisation and yield; consider delivery schedule of owned aircraft and seasonal demand.
