## Likely operations interview questions

1. **"How would you plan supply chain capacity for a sale that does 5x normal volume?"** Forecast by category and region, pre-position stock, flex with 3PL and seasonal labour, add temporary hubs.
2. **"What makes a dark store profitable?"** Orders per day, AOV, basket size, picking time, rider utilisation, rent.
3. **"How would you cut cost per shipment by 10%?"** Route density, load factor, sortation automation, failed-delivery reduction, pin-code level SLAs.
4. **"Should Ekart serve competitors' sellers?"** Utilisation vs capacity conflict at peak; pricing; SLA priority rules.
5. **"How would you reduce failed deliveries?"** Address quality (AI resolution), customer contact, delivery slots, pick-up points.
6. **"What KPIs would you track in a fulfilment centre?"** Dock-to-stock time, pick rate, order cycle time, accuracy, units per labour hour.
