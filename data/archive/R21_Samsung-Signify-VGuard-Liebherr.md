On the factory floor, automated surface mounting and robotic assembly lines reduce labor cost per unit, but capital equipment depreciation and software licensing represent major fixed costs.

**Technology, automation and quality.** Manufacturing lines feature high-speed automated component placement (SMT chips mounted at speeds exceeding tens of thousands of components per hour), automated optical inspection (AOI), X-ray solder joint verification, and automated helium leak detection in refrigerator compressor loops. Advanced supply chain planning uses automated ERP and algorithmic warehouse management systems (WMS) across regional distribution centers to synchronize daily factory dispatch with dealer replenishment pull signals.
