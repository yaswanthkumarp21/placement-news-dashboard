## Operations deep dive

**Technology, automation and quality.** UltraTech 47% green power, electric heavy trucks for mining and clinker; Hindalco battery-grade foil quality; Novelis recycling-based rolling at Bay Minette.

## Likely operations interview questions

1. **"How do you cut logistics cost in cement?"** Shorter lead distance, rail vs road mix, split grinding units near markets, bulk vs bagged.
2. **"Your key supplier's plant burns down. What do you do?"** Activate alternates, allocate scarce supply, communicate with customers, review single-source risk.
3. **"How would you restart a plant after a fire?"** Root-cause analysis, safety sign-off, phased commissioning, quality checks before shipping.
4. **"Why integrate backward into alumina and coal?"** Supply security, cost control, capital trade-off.
5. **"How do you integrate an acquired cement company?"** Brand migration, cost benchmarking, fuel and power changes, procurement synergies.
6. **"What is debottlenecking and how would you do it?"** Find the constraint (kiln, mill, packer), fix it cheaply, re-balance the line.
