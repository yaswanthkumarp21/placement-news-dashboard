- Generic pointer: A big retailer is turning its delivery network into a business that serves others.

### 2026-09-29 | Flipkart adds 50% more warehousing and 900 delivery hubs before Big Billion Days
- Source: https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/consumer-products/Flipkart-Adds-50percent-More-Warehousing-Before-Big-Billion-Days/6abba7675aacb956d084a445 (Whalesbook, 29 Sep 2026)
- Recap: Flipkart added 14 million cubic feet of storage (a 50% increase in national capacity), 900 delivery hubs and 750 new pincodes. New automation should lift package processing by more than 30% in the festive peak. Its quick-commerce arm runs 1,200 micro-fulfilment centres across 150 cities, and it is hiring 2.5 lakh seasonal staff, mostly last-mile.
- Tags: function = supply-chain, logistics | type = expansion
- Confidence: single source (the exact date of Big Billion Days is not stated in it).
- Generic pointer: Flipkart is building capacity ahead of the festive sale.
- Why it matters (Supply Chain / Logistics):
  1. Classic peak-season capacity planning: how do you size warehousing and manpower for a spike that lasts days, and what happens to utilisation afterwards?
  2. Automation vs seasonal labour is a flexibility trade-off: automation raises fixed cost, temporary hiring keeps it variable.
  3. Serving 750 remote pincodes raises cost-to-serve: expect "is expanding reach worth it" and "how would you set up micro-fulfilment for quick commerce" questions.
