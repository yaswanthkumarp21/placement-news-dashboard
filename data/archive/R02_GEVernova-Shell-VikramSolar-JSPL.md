## Likely operations interview questions

1. **"Why integrate backward into cells and wafers?"** Supply security, lower pipeline inventory, DCR eligibility, margin capture; risk is technology obsolescence and ramp yields.
2. **"A new cell line runs at 40% in year one. How would you speed the ramp?"** Yield learning, SPC, tool matching, operator training, early customer qualification.
3. **"How would you cut 60 days of pipeline inventory?"** Domestic cell sourcing, vendor-managed inventory, shorter shipping lanes, better demand signals.
4. **"Which inputs would you hedge?"** Aluminium and copper (35% of RM), then EVA via crude-linked contracts.
5. **"How would you plan production when a policy deadline keeps moving?"** Scenario-based S&OP, flexible DCR/non-DCR mix, staged procurement.
6. **"What does an automated module line change for plant management?"** Fewer operators, more maintenance skill, higher OEE focus, consistent quality.

## Likely operations interview questions

1. **"How would you schedule a planned furnace or BOF reline?"** Pick a low-demand window, build stock, raise output elsewhere, secure refractory and contractors early.
2. **"Your new blast furnace runs at 50%. What limits the ramp?"** Burden quality, coke rate, hot-metal evacuation, downstream capacity, raw material logistics.
3. **"Compare a slurry pipeline with rail for iron ore."** Lower per-tonne cost and emissions, high capex, inflexible route; rail is flexible but costlier and congestion-prone.
4. **"Coking coal is fully imported. How do you manage that risk?"** Blending, multi-country sourcing, term contracts, inventory buffers.
5. **"How do you run a steel plant with under 11 days of inventory?"** Order-driven scheduling, reliable logistics, buffer only at bottlenecks.
6. **"Export or sell domestically?"** Compare netback after freight and duty, customer commitments and safeguard effects.
