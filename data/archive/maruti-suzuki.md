### 2026-05-18 | Second Kharkhoda plant starts commercial production
- Sources: https://www.globalsuzuki.com/globalnews/2026/0518.html (Suzuki Motor, 18 May 2026); https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/maruti-suzuki-starts-production-at-2nd-plant-of-kharkhoda-facility/ (The Tribune, 18 May 2026)
- Recap: The second plant at Kharkhoda (Haryana) began commercial production with 2.5 lakh units a year, taking Kharkhoda to 5 lakh units and Maruti's total installed capacity to 26.5 lakh units. The Tribune says Kharkhoda will reach 10 lakh units when fully built; Suzuki says it aims for 4 million units a year in India over the mid term.
- Tags: function = operations | type = expansion
- Note: the two sources differ on which models the new plant builds, so models are left out.
- Generic pointer: Maruti keeps adding factory capacity in phases.
- Why it matters (Manufacturing / Operations):
  1. Phased capacity addition (2.5 lakh units at a time) is a way to match supply to demand while limiting capital risk: be ready to explain the trade-off against building everything at once.
  2. Clustering plants in Haryana and Gujarat raises questions about location choice, supplier proximity and logistics cost.
  3. Capacity is being added ahead of demand: a good opening for a question on utilisation and break-even volumes.

### 2026-07-30 | Fourth Hansalpur plant starts, taking the Gujarat site to 1 million units a year
- Source: https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/maruti-suzuki-starts-production-at-fourth-hansalpur-plant-hits-1-million-unit-capacity/46945238 (ScanX, 30 Jul 2026)
- Recap: The fourth Hansalpur plant began production, first with the e VITARA electric SUV, at an investment of ₹3,900 crore (cumulative Hansalpur investment ₹25,289 crore). Hansalpur now makes 1 million units a year, total company capacity is 2.9 million, and the site handled nearly 47% of overseas shipments in FY25-26.
- Tags: function = operations | type = expansion
- Confidence: single source.
- Generic pointer: Maruti's export hub in Gujarat grows and starts building an EV.
- Why it matters (Manufacturing / Operations):
  1. Hansalpur's role in exports (nearly 47% of overseas shipments) shows how a plant is chosen as an export base: expect "what makes a good export manufacturing site" questions.
  2. Starting EV production on a new line is a lesson in flexibility: discuss line design for mixed petrol and EV models.
  3. Quote the investment per site (₹25,289 crore cumulative) to show you understand the scale of capital behind capacity.

### 2026-07-31 | Q1 FY27: sales up 29%, but profit falls as material costs rise
- Source: https://www.marutisuzuki.com/corporate/media/press-releases/2026/july/maruti-suzuki-announces-financial-results-for-quarter-1 (Maruti Suzuki press release, 31 Jul 2026)
- Recap: Sales volume grew 29.3% and market share rose 2.3 points to 41.2%. Net sales were ₹49,959 crore (+36%), but net profit fell to ₹3,352 crore from ₹3,758 crore. The company said material costs "had started to increase in the quarter and were seriously aggravated during the war". Network inventory was about 13 days. The Board approved 4 compressed biogas (CBG) projects worth ₹561 crore.
- Tags: function = operations | type = results
- Generic pointer: Maruti sold much more but earned less because inputs got expensive.
- Why it matters (Manufacturing / Operations):
  1. Strong volumes with falling profit is a textbook input-cost story: be ready to discuss procurement, supplier contracts, cost pass-through and how operations can protect margin.
  2. Network inventory of about 13 days is an operations KPI you can cite when asked about inventory turns and dealer stock.
  3. The biogas (CBG) projects link to fuel diversification and sustainability: a possible GD topic on cost versus sustainability in manufacturing.
