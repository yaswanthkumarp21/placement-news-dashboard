## 1. Sector operations overview

1. **E-commerce fulfilment** runs on networks of fulfilment centres, sortation hubs and last-mile hubs. Flipkart's Ekart has 8,200+ facilities and 14,000+ trucks a day; capacity flexes for festive peaks with 3PL partners and seasonal labour. ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/flipkart-ekart-warehousing-capacity-big-billion-days-festive-season-126092900466_1.html))
2. **Quick commerce** uses dark stores (micro-warehouses) within a 2–3 km radius. Economics depend on orders per store per day, average order value, picking speed, rider utilisation and wastage. The market was about ₹11,000 cr in monthly GMV and about 7.8 m orders a day by January 2026. ([The Tech Portal](https://thetechportal.com/2026/09/24/amazon-to-invest-3bn-into-quick-commerce-in-india-as-it-plays-catch-up))
3. **Express and surface logistics** (Allcargo-Gati, FedEx) run hub-and-spoke networks; margin depends on load factor, yield and hub productivity. About 95% of Allcargo's express volume moves by road. ([Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/allcargo-q1fy27-97300))
4. **India's logistics cost** is now officially estimated at **7.97% of GDP** (DPIIT–NCAER, FY24 data), far below the older 13–14% estimates. Road freight costs ₹3.78/tonne-km against ₹1.96 for rail, and fuel is 42% of road freight cost. ([Logistics Insider](https://www.logisticsinsider.in/indias-logistics-cost-at-7-9-of-gdp-report/); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/economy-and-policy/indias-logistics-cost-pegged-at-797-pc-of-gdp-in-first-systematic-study)) *(context; report predates or straddles the window)*
5. **Fintech operations** (PhonePe, Groww) are about uptime, settlement, KYC and fraud at national scale. PhonePe handled 9.28 bn UPI transactions in February 2026. ([Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/phonepe-puts-ipo-on-hold-citing-market-volatility/503467))
6. **Marketplaces** (CarWale, OLX, Amazon sellers like Clicktech) are moving from listing fees to transactions, so they now own fulfilment quality (inspection, delivery, returns).
7. **IT services** (Mphasis) operations are delivery and transition management; AI-led deals change staffing models.
8. **What changed in 2026:** regulation of the last mile (rider safety, gig social security, dark-store hygiene), more capital into quick commerce, export-led e-commerce inventory, and more dependence on third-party cloud.

## 3. Top 10 GD topics for Sector 2

1. **"Is quick commerce sustainable in India?"**
   - **For:** about 7.8 m orders a day; Blinkit EBITDA-positive.
   - **Against:** price war, 1.8% wastage, ₹2.5 cr capex per store, safety crackdowns.
   - **Facts:** Eternal Q1 FY27; Amazon $3 bn; Maharashtra FDA.
2. **"Should 10-minute delivery be banned or regulated?"**
   - **For:** rider safety, and strikes forced change.
   - **Against:** speed can come from store density, not risky riding; consumer choice.
   - **Facts:** January 2026 agreement; gig rules (May 2026).
3. **"Gig-worker social security: fair cost or growth killer?"**
   - **For:** a safety net for millions of workers.
   - **Against:** most workers miss the 90-day threshold; compliance and data burden.
   - **Facts:** Social Security (Central) Rules, 8 May 2026.
4. **"Should FDI e-commerce firms be allowed to hold domestic inventory?"**
   - **For:** efficiency, export boost (Press Note 3 is already a step).
   - **Against:** small-retailer protection; hard to police the export-domestic split.
   - **Facts:** Press Note 3 (2026).
5. **"Dark stores in residential areas: zoning and food-safety rules?"**
   - **For:** hygiene lapses (14 licences suspended).
   - **Against:** over-regulation could kill a job-creating model.
   - **Facts:** Maharashtra FDA, August 2026.
6. **"Is India's logistics cost really 8% of GDP, and what next?"**
   - **For:** the new official methodology; rail at about half the cost of road.
   - **Against:** MSMEs still pay about 17% of output.
   - **Facts:** DPIIT–NCAER study.
7. **"Should the NPCI 30% UPI cap be enforced in December 2026?"**
   - **For:** concentration risk in two apps.
   - **Against:** forcing users off apps they prefer; operational disruption.
   - **Facts:** PhonePe 650 m users; deadline 31 December 2026.
8. **"Profit vs growth: should startups list only after breakeven?"**
   - **For:** investor protection (Walmart's Flipkart directive).
   - **Against:** capital needs for scale; IPO windows close (PhonePe pause).
   - **Facts:** March–May 2026 events.
9. **"Cloud dependence: is India's fintech stack too concentrated?"**
   - **For:** single-vendor outages hit many apps at once.
   - **Against:** multi-cloud is costly; outages are rare and short.
   - **Facts:** August 2026 Cloudflare/GCP outage.
10. **"Air cargo hubs: should India build global transhipment hubs?"**
    - **For:** Gulf disruption showed the risk of relying on foreign hubs; FedEx is investing in Mumbai and Delhi.
    - **Against:** competition from Dubai and Singapore; slot and customs constraints.
    - **Facts:** FedEx ₹2,500 cr Navi Mumbai and $150 m Delhi hubs.
