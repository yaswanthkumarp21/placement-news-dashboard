## Likely operations interview questions

1. **"Why is First-Pass Yield (FPY) the single most critical operational metric in optical lens manufacturing?"**  
   *Answer outline:* Unlike metal parts that can be remelted or machined down, an ophthalmic prescription lens surfaced with a curvature or coating error is chemically cross-linked plastic that cannot be reworked; scrap directly wastes expensive high-index resins and PVD machine chamber time, making 99%+ FPY essential.
2. **"What was the strategic logic behind Carl Zeiss investing ₹2,500 crore in a mega-plant in Bengaluru?"**  
   *Answer outline:* Capitalizes on India’s booming domestic eye care demand (rising myopia and screen time), provides a cost-competitive global manufacturing hub complementing German and Mexican plants, and leverages Bengaluru's precision engineering and software talent cluster.
3. **"How does Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) coating work in a precision lens cleanroom?"**  
   *Answer outline:* Lenses are mounted on rotating planetary carousels inside a high-vacuum chamber; electron beams evaporate metal oxides (silica, zirconia), depositing ultra-thin, nanometer-scale anti-reflective, scratch-resistant, and oleophobic layers onto the lens surface.
4. **"How does Zeiss manage the logistics of delivering personalized progressive eyeglasses within 48 hours?"**  
   *Answer outline:* Digital prescription parameters are uploaded via clinic wavefront scanners, automatically allocated to the nearest automated finishing lab, processed through automated freeform surfacing and express curing, and shipped via dedicated courier linehaul.
5. **"What role do Coordinate Measuring Machines (CMM) play in automotive and aerospace manufacturing?"**  
   *Answer outline:* CMMs use high-precision tactile touch probes and optical lasers on air-bearing granite tables to measure the physical 3D coordinates of manufactured engine blocks and turbine blades, verifying tolerances down to fractions of a micron.
6. **"How does cleanroom environmental control (temperature, humidity, dust) impact optical yield?"**  
   *Answer outline:* Sub-micron dust particles trapped under anti-reflective coatings cause immediate optical pinhole defects; humidity and temperature variations alter the chemical curing rate and index of refraction of liquid monomers during casting.
