### 2026-07-29 | Q1 FY27: revenue up 18%, profit up 40%, decorative volume up 9%
- Source: https://upstox.com/news/market-news/earnings/asian-paints-q1-fy-27-results-net-profit-surges-40-revenue-grows-to-10-542-crore-yo-y/article-197771/ (Upstox, 29 Jul 2026)
- Recap: Revenue was ₹10,542 crore (+18%) and consolidated net profit ₹1,539 crore (+40%). Decorative paints grew 9% in volume and 16.6% in value; EBITDA margin was 20.57%. CEO Amit Syngle credited "measured price increases, an improved mix, formulation and sourcing efficiencies" and said the company would "remain agile" on raw material volatility.
- Tags: function = distribution, sales-ops | type = results
- Note: this article says nothing about distribution or dealers, so no distribution claim is made here.
- Generic pointer: Asian Paints grew faster and earned much more last quarter.
- Why it matters (Distribution Management):
  1. Volume (9%) is growing slower than value (16.6%), so price and mix are doing a lot of the work: be ready to discuss how distribution keeps volumes moving while prices rise.
  2. A 20.57% EBITDA margin shows pricing power; link it to dealer margins and how much of the gain you can share down the channel.
  3. Raw material volatility is the next risk; interviewers may ask how distribution planning copes when costs jump.

### 2026-09-28 | Analysts flag a possible 400 bps gross-margin squeeze in Q2 FY27 as crude and competition rise
- Source: https://retailintel.in/signal/asian-paints-faces-input-cost-squeeze-as-crude-surge-intensi-8446f12f (Retail Intel analysis, 28 Sep 2026)
- Recap: Retail Intel reports a possible 400 basis point sequential gross-margin decline in Q2 FY27, linking it to crude oil rising 70% in 2026 to about $98 a barrel, and to pressure from Birla Opus and JSW Dulux. It also notes the fall may already be priced in and may be temporary, since the company has historically recovered costs through pricing and mix.
- Tags: function = distribution, sales-ops | type = analysis (not a company statement)
- Generic pointer: Rising oil prices and new rivals could squeeze Asian Paints' margins.
- Why it matters (Distribution Management):
  1. A cost shock plus new competitors forces a choice between raising prices and protecting dealer margins: a likely GD question.
  2. Birla Opus is a new rival with its own dealer network: be ready to compare how an incumbent defends its channel (dealer ROI, service levels) against a challenger.
  3. Treat this as analyst opinion, not fact: say "analysts expect" in an interview, and note the counter-argument.
