## Likely operations interview questions

1. **"How does an in-line lubricants blending plant like Taloja prevent cross-contamination between different oil grades?"**  
   *Answer outline:* Deploy automated pipe-clearing "pigging" systems using compressed-air driven polyurethane projectiles that wipe pipe walls completely clean between batches, segregate dedicated manifold lines for synthetic vs mineral oils, and conduct automated inline optical density flushing checks.
2. **"What is the operational function of an LNG boil-off gas (BOG) compressor in a terminal like Hazira?"**  
   *Answer outline:* Cryogenic LNG stored at $-162^{\circ}\text{C}$ continuously absorbs ambient thermal heat, causing a fraction of liquid to vaporize into boil-off gas; BOG compressors capture this gas, re-compress it, and either re-condense it back to liquid or inject it directly into the high-pressure sendout gas pipeline to prevent flaring and pressure build-up.
3. **"How does Shell manage the supply chain risk of base oil procurement when crude oil prices fluctuate?"**  
   *Answer outline:* SCM utilizes rolling forward supply contracts with domestic and Middle Eastern refiners, maintains 30–45 days of strategic base oil buffer inventory in bonded shore tanks, utilizes formulaic raw-material-linked price escalation clauses with B2B clients, and optimizes blending recipes within approved OEM performance boundaries.
4. **"What are the operational challenges of distributing LNG via cryogenic trucks compared to pipeline transport?"**  
   *Answer outline:* Demands double-walled vacuum-insulated cryogenic road tankers, specialized cryogenic loading arm safety procedures with emergency shutoff (ESD) valves, strict route hazards management, and rapid delivery scheduling to prevent truck venting from pressure rise during transit.
5. **"How does a service like Shell LubeAnalyst create an operational moat for an industrial lubricants supplier?"**  
   *Answer outline:* By analyzing spectral wear-metals and viscosity deterioration in used customer oil, Shell predicts component failure before breakdown occurs, locking commercial clients into long-term supply contracts based on total cost of equipment uptime rather than commodity oil price per liter.
6. **"Why is a hybrid wind-solar PPA operationally superior to standalone solar for a continuous-process plant like Hazira?"**  
   *Answer outline:* Solar power peaks during midday, whereas wind power generation often peaks in evening and night hours; combining solar and wind generation profiles provides a much flatter, round-the-clock green generation curve matching continuous cryogenic pump base-load power demands.

   *Answer outline:* T+0 eliminates the overnight buffer; cash must be collected and reconciled instantly from client UPI/net-banking, securities must be immediately blocked and transferred in the demat account, and real-time fund transfers to the clearing corporation must occur by 4:30 PM on the trade day itself.
