## Likely operations interview questions

1. **"How would you decide when to add capacity on a line running near full?"** Use Bosch's buffer rule; add that equipment lead time and demand forecast confidence set how big the buffer must be.
2. **"Imports from the parent are rising even though you are localising. Explain."** New products start as imports, then assembly, then component localisation; a volume surge in new products temporarily raises the import share.
3. **"Name three ways to cut material cost without hurting quality."** Localise (with PPAP and validation), renegotiate (index-linked contracts), redesign (value engineering); mention validation lead time as the constraint.
4. **"Your JV partner controls procurement. How do you protect quality?"** Joint supplier approval, shared quality KPIs, audit rights and clear 8D ownership for field failures.
5. **"How would you reduce rare-earth risk for an e-axle programme?"** Dual-source magnets, qualify magnet-light designs, hold strategic safety stock, track the government magnet scheme for local supply.
6. **"How would you improve OEE on an assembly line without large capex?"** Break down losses into availability, performance and quality; use SMED, error-proofing and line balancing; cite Bidadi's low-cost kaizen.
