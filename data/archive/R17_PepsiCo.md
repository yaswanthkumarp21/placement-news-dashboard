**Technology, automation and quality.** AI in supply-chain and transport optimisation; digital ordering; farm digital tools; 97% biomass fuel; EV logistics.

## Likely operations interview questions

1. **"How do you secure potato supply all year for a chips plant?"** Contract farming, varieties for processing, cold storage, multiple growing regions.
2. **"When should you forward-buy sugar or PET?"** Price outlook, storage capacity, working capital, shelf life, hedge alternatives.
3. **"Why build regional snack plants?"** Bulky, light product; freight cost per unit; freshness; demand proximity.
4. **"How would you decide which plant to close?"** Utilisation, cost per case, age, capex need, distance to demand, labour.
5. **"Combine food and beverage logistics: benefits and risks?"** Truck fill by weight and volume, fewer drops; risks are complexity and different service needs.
6. **"How do you plan beverage capacity for a weather-driven peak?"** Pre-build inventory, flexible shifts, co-packers, weather-linked forecasts.
