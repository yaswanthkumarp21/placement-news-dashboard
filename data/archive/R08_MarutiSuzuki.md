## Likely operations interview questions

1. **"Demand exceeds capacity by 20%. How do you allocate production?"** Model profitability, market share risk, dealer stock norms, export commitments, waiting-period limits.
2. **"How would you ramp a new 250,000-unit plant?"** Supplier readiness, line balancing, quality gates, staged shifts, workforce training.
3. **"Suppliers ask for price increases during a commodity spike. What do you do?"** Index-linked pricing, faster settlement, cost-down projects, protect weak suppliers.
4. **"Road vs rail for car dispatch?"** Distance, lane volume, terminal access, transit time, damage rates, cost per car.
5. **"Why build a supplier park next to the plant?"** Lower inbound logistics cost, JIT, faster problem-solving, lower inventory.
6. **"How do you limit a recall?"** Batch traceability, supplier lot codes, VIN mapping, root-cause analysis.
